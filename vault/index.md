# Group Lab Archive

This is the group's single place for **every artifact worth keeping**: CAD drawings, microwave simulations, cavity characterizations (mode maps, bead-pulls), raw and processed data, analyses, technical memos, publication drafts, photos and group-meeting viewgraphs. Each one has a **record** saying who made it, when, how, and under what conditions.

> [!warning] Demonstration archive
> All people's roles are illustrative and **all data, drawings and results here are synthetic**, generated by `tools/demo/generate_synthetic_data.py`. The structure is the real proposal.

## How the archive works

1. **Everything is a Markdown note** in an Obsidian vault, stored in a GitHub repository. Git keeps the full history of every change, and whoever made it.
2. **Each record carries structured metadata** (the "Properties" at the top of a note). The required fields for each record type are defined in [[POL-003 Required metadata]] and **checked automatically** on every upload.
3. **Files are named from their record ID**, so a CSV found on a laptop years from now still points back to the note that explains it. See [[POL-002 Naming conventions]].
4. **Every note has an access tier**: `internal`, `collaboration` or `public`. One vault is automatically split into three websites. See [[POL-001 Access tiers]].
5. **Large raw data stays on group storage and public releases get a DOI.** The archive records where they are and their checksums. See [[POL-005 Where files live]] and [[POL-004 Data management and sharing]].
